## How to use this file

1. Until a rubric is verified, the EU baseline applies: [eu.md](eu.md). Tell the user, in the report, which national rubrics were not checked.
2. Never fill a rubric from memory, from a secondary source or from a translation. To fill one: read the official source of this country in its language, cite its URL and the date here, propose the source for `../sources.json`, and update the coverage line (`not verified`, `partial`, `complete`).
3. A country is `complete` only when every rubric cites an official source of this country.
4. Legal labels in the national language (order button, withdrawal function) are never translated freely from English: they come from the national text.
