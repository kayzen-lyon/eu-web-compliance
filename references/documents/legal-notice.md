# Legal notice (mentions légales, imprint): drafting

How to draft or fill the identification page without inventing anything. The lists of what must appear are in [../consumer/operator-identification.md](../consumer/operator-identification.md); this file adds the drafting traps, by operator type. Templates: [legal-notice-fr.md](../../templates/consumer/legal-notice-fr.md) (operator established in France) and [legal-notice-eu.md](../../templates/consumer/legal-notice-eu.md).

## How to use this file

1. Qualify the operator first (section 1). The legal status decides the block of the template, not the kind of number the user gave.
2. Collect the items of section 2 in one message.
3. Fill the template; apply section 3 to every value. The French penalty for missing identification is in [../regulatory-status.md](../regulatory-status.md) section 5.1; do not restate it in the page.

## 1. Qualify the operator

- [ ] Confirm the legal status with the owner (company, sole trader, association, foreign company, private person) before choosing a block; a SIREN or SIRET does not prove that the operator is a commercial company, since associations and sole traders have one too [best practice]
- [ ] A legal person established in France gives its name, registered office and telephone number, and, if registered, the registration number, share capital and registered office address [LCEN art. 1-1]
- [ ] A natural person acting professionally in France gives surname, first names, home address and telephone number, and the registration number if registered [LCEN art. 1-1]
- [ ] An association uses the legal person block without share capital; a number from the national register of associations is not a trade register number, and the two are not confused [best practice]
- [ ] A non-professional may publish only the host's details, if they gave their identity to the host; ask before using that form [LCEN art. 1-1]
- [ ] An operator established in another Member State uses the EU template and its country file; an operator outside the EU is a case to confirm with a professional ([../consumer/operator-identification.md](../consumer/operator-identification.md) section 3) [best practice]
- [ ] An association or another non-commercial body that sells to the public is not exempt from consumer law because of its status; ask what it sells and to whom [best practice]

## 2. What to collect

- Name and legal form; share capital (companies); registered office or establishment address.
- Register and number exactly as written on the register extract, including the city of the register when the register has one.
- VAT identification number, only if the operator is subject to VAT and gives it.
- Telephone number and email address.
- Publication director: a natural person named by the owner (France).
- Host: name, address and telephone number of the company that stores the site, as stated in the hosting contract.
- Regulated activity: authority, professional body, title, rules (only if the owner confirms the activity is regulated).
- Consumer mediator (France, sales to consumers): name and addresses, from the owner's membership.

## 3. Values the agent never makes up

- [ ] The city of the trade register is never deduced from the current registered office: a company may be registered elsewhere, for example after moving; ask, or mark it to be checked on the register extract [best practice]
- [ ] A registration number is copied as given; a SIRET (establishment) is not presented as a SIREN (company) or the reverse; if only a SIRET is given and a SIREN is needed, ask, or show the derived number marked "to confirm" [best practice]
- [ ] The VAT number is shown only when given and confirmed as applicable; it is never computed from another number [ECD art. 5(1)(g)]
- [ ] France: the telephone number is part of the identification of both natural and legal persons; it is not treated as optional [LCEN art. 1-1]
- [ ] The host is the company named in the hosting contract, confirmed by the owner; not the registrar, the CDN, the framework vendor or the commercial name of a product; its legal name and address are never filled with a plausible value [LCEN art. 1-1]
- [ ] When the site and the application or database run on different providers, ask which one stores the site, and record the others as processors in the privacy policy rather than as hosts [best practice]
- [ ] The host is checked again at each change of provider; an old host left on the page is a wrong statement [best practice]
- [ ] The publication director is a natural person named by the owner, even for a one-person company [LCEN art. 1-1]
- [ ] The mediator is chosen by the owner, never by the agent [C. conso R. 616-1]

## 4. What the page may also hold

These blocks are not identification items. Keep them short, and never present them as required by the identification rules.

- [ ] A link to the privacy policy and, where one is designated, the data protection officer's contact; the GDPR content itself stays in the privacy policy [best practice]
- [ ] An intellectual property paragraph, only if the owner wants one; it is a statement of the owner's position, not a legal requirement of the page [best practice]
- [ ] No link to the former EU online dispute resolution platform [ODR repeal]
- [ ] No obligation of an app store or a vendor presented as a legal mention [best practice]

## 5. Review checklist

- [ ] The block used matches the operator's confirmed status [LCEN art. 1-1]
- [ ] Every value comes from the owner; every missing one is a visible placeholder listed in the answer [best practice]
- [ ] The same name, address, host and contact appear in the legal notice, the privacy policy and the terms [best practice]
- [ ] The footer of every page links to the page ([../consumer/operator-identification.md](../consumer/operator-identification.md) section 2) [ECD art. 5(1)]
