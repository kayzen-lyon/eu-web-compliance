Use of artificial intelligence on this site

Last updated: 14 November 2026.

The Northfield Courier uses artificial intelligence (AI) for the features listed below. This page explains where you will meet it, how we tell you, and how to reach a person.

Our AI archive assistant

The chat assistant on the archive page is an AI system, not a person. It finds articles and summarises them; its answers can be wrong, so always read the article it links to. If you ask whether you are talking to a person, it tells you that it is an AI.

Images made or changed with AI

  • AI Generated: the image was created by AI.
  • AI Modified: a real photo was changed with AI; the caption says what was changed.

Articles and texts

Some articles are drafted with AI and then reviewed and fact-checked by our editor before publication. Editorial responsibility: see our newsroom.

Some texts are generated by AI without editorial review. They are labelled "AI Generated" under their title.

Translations of articles are made with an AI translation tool and reviewed by our editor.

What we do not do

  • We do not use AI to make decisions about you.
  • We do not analyse your face, your voice or your emotions.
