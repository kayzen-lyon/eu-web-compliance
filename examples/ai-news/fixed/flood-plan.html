River flood plan goes to public consultation

AI Generated

This article was written by an AI system from the council's published documents and has not been reviewed by an editor. How we use AI

Published 14 November 2026

The district council has opened a six-week public consultation on its plan to build flood barriers along the east bank of the river, between the old bridge and the sports ground.

According to the plan, the barriers would protect about 240 homes. Residents can comment online or at the town hall until 26 December 2026.

The council will publish a summary of the comments before it decides on the project in spring 2027.
