Our services

Every piece is drawn, cut and fitted by our own team of five. Typical costs and lead times are listed in the price guide.

Our founder trained in Normandy and brought the workshop motto back with her: « Le bois a toujours le dernier mot », which means “the wood always has the last word”.

  • Fitted kitchens

    Shaker and plain-fronted kitchens in oak, ash or painted tulipwood, built around the room rather than around standard units.

    Kitchen prices and lead times

  • Staircases

    Straight, winder and open-tread staircases, with handrails turned in our own workshop and fitted in two days.

    Staircase prices and lead times

  • Restoration

    Sash windows, panelled doors and period mouldings repaired with matching timber instead of being replaced.

    Restoration prices and lead times
