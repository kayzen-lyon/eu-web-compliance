Cookies and other trackers

Lindenmoor Tea Company and the companies listed in our cookie policy would like to use trackers on your device to:

  • Audience measurement: count visits and see which pages are read, to improve the shop.
  • Marketing: measure the effect of our advertising on a social network and show you our offers there.
  • Videos from external platforms: play videos hosted by an external video platform, which sets its own trackers.

You can accept or refuse all of them, or choose purpose by purpose. Refusing does not prevent you from using the site: external videos are then replaced by a link. You can change your mind at any time with "Manage cookies" at the bottom of every page. Cookie policy and list of companies using trackers on this site

Choose by purpose

Your choice applies to this browser. It is kept for 6 months, then we ask again. If you withdraw a consent you gave earlier, the page reloads to stop the services concerned.

Audience measurement

Lindenmoor Tea Company uses an audience measurement service to count visits and see which pages are read. Data: pages viewed, approximate device type. Company involved: the analytics provider listed in the cookie policy.

Marketing

We measure which of our advertisements on a social network lead to visits and orders, and the network may show you our offers. Data: pages viewed, products added to the basket, orders. Company involved: the social network listed in the cookie policy, which also uses the data for its own purposes.

Videos from external platforms

Videos are played by an external video platform, which sets trackers on your device and may use them for its own purposes. Without your consent, a link to the video is shown instead.

Trackers that are strictly necessary (keeping your choice, your basket, your session) do not need your consent. They are listed in the cookie policy.
