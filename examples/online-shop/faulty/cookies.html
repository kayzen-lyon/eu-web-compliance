Cookies and other trackers

Last updated: 2026-11-14

When you visit this shop, information may be stored on, or read from, your device. This happens through cookies and similar techniques such as local storage or pixels. We call all of them "trackers" here. This page explains which ones we use, why, and how you control them.

Your choices

Trackers that are not strictly necessary are used only if you agree. When you first visit, a banner lets you accept all of them, refuse all of them, or choose purpose by purpose. Refusing does not prevent you from using the shop.

Your choice, whether you accept or refuse, is kept for 6 months. After that, or if we add a new purpose or partner, we ask you again.

You can change your mind at any time with the "Manage cookies" button at the bottom of every page, or here:

If you withdraw your consent, we delete the trackers we set ourselves for that purpose and stop loading the services concerned. Trackers set by other companies on their own domains must be deleted from your browser settings.

Marketing
