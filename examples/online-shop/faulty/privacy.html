Cookies and other trackers

Lindenmoor Tea Company and the companies listed in our cookie policy would like to use trackers on your device to:

  • Audience measurement: count visits and see which pages are read, to improve the shop.
  • Marketing: measure the effect of our advertising on a social network and show you our offers there.
  • Videos from external platforms: play videos hosted by an external video platform, which sets its own trackers.

You can accept or refuse all of them, or choose purpose by purpose. Refusing does not prevent you from using the site: external videos are then replaced by a link. Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-14

This page explains which personal data Lindenmoor Tea Company collects through this shop, why, and what rights you have. Lindenmoor Tea Company is a fictional business: this policy is an example, not legal advice.

Who is responsible for your data

The controller is Lindenmoor Tea Company Ltd (fictional), 4 Harbour Row, 0000 Exampletown. Contact: privacy@lindenmoor-tea.example.

What we collect, why, and on which basis

Orders and invoices

  • Data: name, delivery address, email address, order details. This example shop has no payment provider and stores no card number.
  • Purpose: processing and delivering your order, invoicing, after-sales service.
  • Lawful basis: performance of the contract, and legal obligations for invoices.
  • Retention: for the duration of the order and after-sales service, then for the legal retention period for invoices.

Your basket

  • Data: the products you put in your basket, kept in your browser only.
  • Purpose: keeping your basket between visits.
  • Lawful basis: performance of the contract you are preparing.
  • Retention: until you empty the basket or clear your browser data.

Audience measurement and marketing

  • Data: pages viewed, products added to the basket, orders, approximate device type, online identifiers.
  • Purpose: measuring the audience of the shop, and measuring and targeting our advertising on a social network.
  • Lawful basis: your consent, given in the cookie banner, which you can withdraw at any time with "Manage cookies".
  • Retention: see the cookie policy.

Security and technical logs

  • Data: IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type.
  • Purpose: keeping the shop secure and working.
  • Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in securing the shop.
  • Retention: 6 months.

Fields of the checkout form are required unless marked "optional": without them we cannot deliver your order.

Transfers outside the European Union

The audience measurement provider, the social network and the video platform may process data outside the European Union. These transfers rely on the providers' certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You can obtain more information by writing to privacy@lindenmoor-tea.example.

Cookies and other trackers

Our use of cookies and other trackers, and how to change your choices, is explained in our cookie policy. You can change your choices at any time with "Manage cookies" at the bottom of every page.

Automated decisions

We do not take decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and obtain a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased, in the cases provided by law;
  • restrict the processing of your data;
  • receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format, and have them sent to another organisation, when processing is based on your consent or a contract;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and object at any time to direct marketing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

To exercise your rights, write to privacy@lindenmoor-tea.example. We answer within the time limit set by Article 12 of the General Data Protection Regulation.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the Member State where you live or work.

Changes to this policy

Your choice applies to this browser. It is kept for 6 months, then we ask again. If you withdraw a consent you gave earlier, the page reloads to stop the services concerned.

Audience measurement

Lindenmoor Tea Company uses an audience measurement service to count visits and see which pages are read. Data: pages viewed, approximate device type. Company involved: the analytics provider listed in the cookie policy.

Marketing

We measure which of our advertisements on a social network lead to visits and orders, and the network may show you our offers. Data: pages viewed, products added to the basket, orders. Company involved: the social network listed in the cookie policy, which also uses the data for its own purposes.

Videos from external platforms

Videos are played by an external video platform, which sets trackers on your device and may use them for its own purposes. Without your consent, a link to the video is shown instead.

Trackers that are strictly necessary (keeping your choice, your basket, your session) do not need your consent. They are listed in the cookie policy.
