Cookies and other trackers

Larkspur Reading Circle and the companies listed in our cookie policy would like to use trackers on your device to:

  • Audience measurement: count visits and see which pages are read, to improve the service.
  • Support chat: offer a chat with our volunteers, run by an external help-desk provider that sets its own trackers.

You can accept or refuse all of them, or choose purpose by purpose. Refusing does not prevent you from using the site: the chat is then replaced by an email address. You can change your mind at any time with "Manage cookies" at the bottom of every page. Cookie policy and list of companies using trackers on this site

Choose by purpose

Your choice applies to this browser. It is kept for 6 months, then we ask again. If you withdraw a consent you gave earlier, the page reloads to stop the services concerned.

Audience measurement

Larkspur Reading Circle uses an audience measurement service to count visits and see which pages are read. Data: pages viewed, approximate device type. Company involved: the analytics provider listed in the cookie policy.

Support chat

A chat window lets you ask our volunteers for help. It is run by an external help-desk provider, which sets trackers to keep the conversation open between pages. Without your consent, write to help@larkspur-reading.example instead.

Trackers that are strictly necessary (keeping your choice, your basket, your session) do not need your consent. They are listed in the cookie policy.
