Cookies and other trackers

Last updated: 2026-11-14

When you use this site, information may be stored on, or read from, your device. This happens through cookies and similar techniques such as local storage. We call all of them "trackers" here. This page explains which ones we use, why, and how you control them.

Your choices

Trackers that are not strictly necessary are used only if you agree. When you first visit, a banner lets you accept all of them, refuse all of them, or choose purpose by purpose. Refusing does not prevent you from using the site.

Your choice, whether you accept or refuse, is kept for 6 months. After that, or if we add a new purpose or partner, we ask you again.

You can change your mind at any time with the "Manage cookies" button at the bottom of every page, or here:

If you withdraw your consent, we delete the trackers we set ourselves for that purpose and stop loading the services concerned. Trackers set by other companies on their own domains must be deleted from your browser settings.

Larkspur Reading Circle uses an audience measurement service to count visits and see which pages are read. Data: pages viewed, approximate device type. Company involved: the analytics provider listed in the cookie policy.

Support chat

A chat window lets you ask our volunteers for help. It is run by an external help-desk provider, which sets trackers to keep the conversation open between pages. Without your consent, write to help@larkspur-reading.example instead.

Trackers that are strictly necessary (keeping your choice, your basket, your session) do not need your consent. They are listed in the cookie policy.
