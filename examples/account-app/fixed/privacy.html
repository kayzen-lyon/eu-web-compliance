Cookies and other trackers

Larkspur Reading Circle and the companies listed in our cookie policy would like to use trackers on your device to:

  • Audience measurement: count visits and see which pages are read, to improve the service.
  • Support chat: offer a chat with our volunteers, run by an external help-desk provider that sets its own trackers.

You can accept or refuse all of them, or choose purpose by purpose. Refusing does not prevent you from using the site: the chat is then replaced by an email address. Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-14

This page explains which personal data Larkspur Reading Circle collects through this site, why, and what rights you have. Larkspur Reading Circle is a fictional association: this policy is an example, not legal advice.

Who is responsible for your data

The controller is Larkspur Reading Circle (fictional association), 1 Example Street, 00000 Exampletown. Contact: privacy@larkspur-reading.example.

What we collect, why, and on which basis

Your account

  • Data: name, email address, password (stored hashed), postal address, books borrowed.
  • Purpose: creating and managing your membership and your loans.
  • Lawful basis: performance of the membership agreement.
  • Retention: as long as you are a member, then deleted within one month, except loans not yet returned.

Newsletter

  • Data: email address.
  • Purpose: sending you the monthly newsletter, if you asked for it.
  • Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link in each email or in your account.
  • Retention: until you unsubscribe.

Support chat

  • Data: your messages and the identifiers set by the chat provider.
  • Purpose: answering your questions.
  • Lawful basis: your consent, given in the cookie banner.
  • Retention: 12 months after the conversation.

Security and technical logs

  • Data: IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type, sign-in attempts.
  • Purpose: keeping the service secure and working, and protecting accounts against password guessing.
  • Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in securing the service.
  • Retention: 6 months.

Fields marked "required" in our forms are needed to create and run your account. Other fields are optional.

Who receives your data

Your data are accessible only to the volunteers of the circle who need them, and to the following service providers acting on our behalf:

Transfers outside the European Union

The audience measurement provider may process data outside the European Union, if you accept audience measurement. This transfer relies on the provider's certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Your account data are not transferred outside the European Union.

Cookies and other trackers

Our use of cookies and other trackers, and how to change your choices, is explained in our cookie policy. You can change your choices at any time with "Manage cookies" at the bottom of every page.

Automated decisions

We do not take decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and obtain a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased, in the cases provided by law;
  • restrict the processing of your data;
  • receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format, and have them sent to another organisation, when processing is based on your consent or a contract;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and object at any time to direct marketing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

You can download or delete your data directly from your account.

To exercise your rights, write to privacy@larkspur-reading.example. We answer within the time limit set by Article 12 of the General Data Protection Regulation.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the Member State where you live or work.

Changes to this policy

We update this page when our processing changes. The date at the top shows the last update.

Larkspur Reading Circle uses an audience measurement service to count visits and see which pages are read. Data: pages viewed, approximate device type. Company involved: the analytics provider listed in the cookie policy.

Support chat

A chat window lets you ask our volunteers for help. It is run by an external help-desk provider, which sets trackers to keep the conversation open between pages. Without your consent, write to help@larkspur-reading.example instead.

Trackers that are strictly necessary (keeping your choice, your basket, your session) do not need your consent. They are listed in the cookie policy.
