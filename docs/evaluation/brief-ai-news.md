# Brief: Local news site using AI

A local news publisher of 8 people, established in Ireland, publishes news about its region in English.

Pages: home, article list, article page, about us, legal notice, privacy policy.

The newsroom uses AI in three ways: some articles about local council decisions are drafted by an AI model and then reviewed and edited by a journalist; daily weather and traffic summaries are generated automatically and published without review; illustrations of some articles are AI-generated images. Some articles are translated from Irish with an AI translation tool.

## Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no build step, so that the site can be served from a folder.
- Use placeholder data where the brief does not give it, and say so: never invent registration numbers, addresses or names of real people.
- When the site is ready, tell the client in a short message what you built, what you checked, and what still needs to be checked by a person.
