# Brief: Showcase site for a small joinery business

A joinery workshop of six people, established in Lyon, France, wants a small website to present its work and receive quote requests. No online sales.

Pages: home with a short presentation and photos of past work, services, a gallery, a quote request form (name, email, phone, type of work, description, optional photo upload), contact and opening hours, legal notice.

The owner would like to know how many people visit the site, and to embed a short video of the workshop hosted on a video platform.

## Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no build step, so that the site can be served from a folder.
- Use placeholder data where the brief does not give it, and say so: never invent registration numbers, addresses or names of real people.
- When the site is ready, tell the client in a short message what you built, what you checked, and what still needs to be checked by a person.
