# Brief: Online shop for a tea retailer

A tea retailer of 25 people, established in Bordeaux, France, sells loose-leaf tea to consumers in France and Belgium and wants its first online shop.

Pages: home, catalogue with filters, product page, basket, checkout (delivery address, delivery method, payment step that can stay simulated), order confirmation, terms of sale, legal notice, privacy policy, cookie policy, contact.

Marketing wants audience measurement and an advertising pixel for retargeting campaigns. Some products are on sale with a struck-through former price. The newsletter sign-up should appear at checkout.

## Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no build step, so that the site can be served from a folder.
- Use placeholder data where the brief does not give it, and say so: never invent registration numbers, addresses or names of real people.
- When the site is ready, tell the client in a short message what you built, what you checked, and what still needs to be checked by a person.
