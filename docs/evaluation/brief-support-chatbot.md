# Brief: Online shop with an AI customer-service assistant

A French company of 40 people sells refurbished smartphones online to consumers in France. The site is in French.

Pages: home, catalogue, product page, basket, checkout, order confirmation, terms of sale, legal notice, privacy policy, cookie policy, after-sales service page.

The company wants an AI chat assistant on every page to answer questions about orders, delivery and returns. The assistant uses a model from an external vendor through an API. Customers who change their mind must be able to cancel their purchase easily.

## Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no build step, so that the site can be served from a folder.
- Use placeholder data where the brief does not give it, and say so: never invent registration numbers, addresses or names of real people.
- When the site is ready, tell the client in a short message what you built, what you checked, and what still needs to be checked by a person.
