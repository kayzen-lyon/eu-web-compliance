# Template: terms of use (conditions générales d'utilisation), French

Guidance for the agent. Everything above the line "PUBLISHABLE TEXT" stays out of the published document.

This template is produced with an AI tool. It is not legal advice. The published text binds the operator: the operator and their lawyer read and approve it before it goes online. Say so to the user when you hand it over, with the disclaimer of [../../references/documents/README.md](../../references/documents/README.md) section 5.

## When to use it

When the site has user accounts (`site.user_accounts`) or an online service, and always when it stores content supplied by its users (`content.hosts_user_content`): the Digital Services Act then requires the moderation rules in the terms. Rules: [../../references/documents/terms-of-use.md](../../references/documents/terms-of-use.md) and [../../references/consumer/dsa.md](../../references/consumer/dsa.md). Paid offers go in the terms of sale ([terms-of-sale-fr.md](terms-of-sale-fr.md)); a combined "conditions générales d'utilisation et de vente" keeps the sale part as a separate section.

## What comes from the operator, never from the agent

Operator identity (copy from the legal notice), description of the service, access conditions and minimum age, forbidden conduct, rights the operator needs over user content, moderation measures that really exist, suspension grounds and procedure, liability wording, how changes are notified. Each stays a `[placeholder]` until supplied; list every remaining one in the answer.

## What the agent must never write

- An acceptance mechanism that the operator did not confirm. Article 2 carries a conditional bracket; recommend a separate unchecked checkbox at sign-up in the answer, as a recommendation.
- A moderation tool, filter, response time or appeal step that does not exist.
- An exclusion of all liability, a change of the terms "à tout moment sans préavis", an exclusive court against consumers, a governing-law clause that removes the consumer's protection.
- An app store or sign-in provider rule presented as a legal obligation.
- Anything about personal data beyond the link to the privacy policy.

## Conditional clauses in this template

| Clause | Condition written in the document | Delete when |
|--------|-----------------------------------|-------------|
| 2 Acceptance | The mechanism confirmed by the operator | Never: replace the bracket by the confirmed mechanism |
| 3.2 Minimum age | The operator sets one | The service has no age condition (then say so to the owner: minors are a stop-and-refer case if they use it) |
| 5 User content and 6 Moderation | The service hosts user content | It does not |
| 6.4 Minors | The service is mainly used by or aimed at minors | It is not |
| 9 Accessibility | The service is in scope of the European Accessibility Act | It is not |
