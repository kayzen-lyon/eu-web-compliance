# Template: "Use of AI on this site" page

Guidance for the agent. Everything above the line "PUBLISHABLE TEXT" stays out of the published page.

This template is produced with an AI tool. It is not legal advice. The published text speaks for the site owner: the owner, or their lawyer, reads and approves it before it goes online. Say so to the user when you hand it over.

## When to use it

When the site uses at least one AI feature that visitors meet: a chatbot, AI-generated or AI-modified content, a generator offered to visitors. The page is not required by the AI Act as such, and it never replaces the notices at the point of use: a disclosure that exists only on a separate page is not enough for Article 50 ([../../references/ai-act/transparency.md](../../references/ai-act/transparency.md) section E). It is the place the chat notice and the content labels link to for details, and the Code of Practice encourages deployers to publish what their labels mean (same file, section D).

## What you must never invent

- A human review that does not happen ("all articles are checked by our editors") or a reviewer's name.
- A model or vendor name, a hosting country, a retention period.
- A claim that the site "complies with the AI Act", is "certified", or that the AI "never makes mistakes".
- A promise of human contact that the operator does not offer.

Write it in the language of the site, in plain words, with headings and lists so it is easy to scan. The page follows the accessibility rules of the rest of the site.
